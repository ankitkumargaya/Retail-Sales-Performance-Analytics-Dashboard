# 📊 Analysis & Methodology

This folder documents the analytical approach, methodology, and insights generated from the retail sales data.

---

## 🧭 Analytical Framework

The analysis follows a **6-phase approach** to generate actionable business insights:

### **Phase 1: Data Preparation & Cleaning**
- Consolidated data from multiple sources  
- Standardized date formats and numeric values  
- Removed duplicates and handled missing values  
- Validated data quality and integrity  

### **Phase 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
- Summarized key statistics and distributions  
- Identified outliers and anomalies  
- Analyzed missing data patterns  
- Established baseline metrics  

### **Phase 3: Dimensional Analysis**
- Regional performance breakdown  
- Product category analysis  
- Customer segment evaluation  
- Delivery performance assessment  

### **Phase 4: Correlation & Impact Analysis**
- Delivery performance vs Customer satisfaction  
- Product margin vs Sales volume  
- Regional profitability drivers  
- Seasonal patterns and trends  

### **Phase 5: Insights Generation**
- Identified key performance drivers  
- Highlighted outliers and anomalies  
- Uncovered business opportunities  
- Prioritized improvement areas  

### **Phase 6: Recommendations & Action Planning**
- Developed actionable recommendations  
- Quantified expected business impact  
- Created implementation roadmap  
- Established success metrics

## 📊 Key Metrics & KPIs Defined

### **Revenue Metrics**
- **Total Revenue** = Sum of all order amounts  
- **Revenue by Region** = Sum of orders grouped by region  
- **Revenue by Category** = Sum of orders grouped by product category  
- **Revenue Growth %** = (Current Period - Previous Period) / Previous Period × 100  

### **Profitability Metrics**
- **Total Profit** = Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold  
- **Profit Margin %** = (Profit / Revenue) × 100  
- **Profit by Region** = Sum of profit grouped by region  
- **Profit Margin by Category** = (Category Profit / Category Revenue) × 100  

### **Operational Metrics**
- **On-Time Delivery Rate** = (On-time deliveries / Total deliveries) × 100  
- **Average Delivery Days** = Average of (Delivery Date - Order Date)  
- **Late Delivery Rate** = (Late deliveries / Total deliveries) × 100  
- **Early Delivery Rate** = (Early deliveries / Total deliveries) × 100  

### **Customer Satisfaction Metrics**
- **Average Rating** = Sum of ratings / Number of ratings  
- **Rating Distribution** = Count of ratings by score (1-5)  
- **Satisfaction Score** = Weighted average (4-5 stars = Satisfied)  
- **Satisfaction by Region** = Average rating grouped by region  

### **Sales Metrics**
- **Total Transactions** = Count of unique orders  
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** = Total Revenue / Total Transactions  
- **Units Sold** = Sum of quantities ordered  
- **Transaction Frequency** = Orders per customer (average)  

### **Customer Metrics**
- **Total Customers** = Count of unique customer IDs  
- **New Customers** = Customers acquired in current period  
- **Repeat Customer Rate** = (Customers with 2+ orders / Total customers) × 100  
- **Customer Retention Rate** = (Retained customers / Previous period customers) × 100
